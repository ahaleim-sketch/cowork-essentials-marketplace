# Trend Scanner

Find what is trending in your niche right now and surface the best content opportunities with angles, hooks, and a clear recommendation.

## Instructions

### Step 1: Identify the User's Niche

Check CLAUDE.md for the user's niche, audience, content style, and platform focus. If this information exists, use it and move on.

If no niche is defined, ask:
> "What niche should I scan for trends? (e.g., AI tools, fitness, personal finance, cooking, SaaS, gaming)"

Do not proceed without a clear niche.

### Step 2: Deep Trend Research

Use WebSearch to scan across all of these categories. Run at least 5-6 searches to build a complete picture.

**Categories to scan:**

1. **Social media buzz** — What topics in this niche are getting unusual engagement on Twitter/X, TikTok, Instagram, Reddit, and YouTube in the last 48 hours? Look for spikes, not steady performers.

2. **News and launches** — Any product launches, feature updates, funding rounds, acquisitions, or major announcements in this space? Check tech news, niche publications, and press releases.

3. **Competitor activity** — What are the top 5-10 creators in this niche posting about right now? What is getting the most views, comments, and shares? Look for patterns — if multiple creators are covering the same thing, it is trending.

4. **Search trends** — What are people searching for in this niche? Look for rising queries, "breakout" terms, and seasonal spikes. Check what autocomplete suggestions are appearing.

5. **Community chatter** — What questions are people asking in Reddit communities, Facebook groups, Discord servers, and forums related to this niche? Repeated questions signal content demand.

6. **Upcoming catalysts** — Any events, product launches, deadlines, or seasonal moments in the next 7-14 days that will drive attention to this niche?

Collect at least 8-10 raw findings before moving to scoring.

### Step 3: Score Each Finding

Rate every finding across 6 criteria:

| Criteria | Scale | What It Measures |
|---|---|---|
| Buzz | 1-5 | How much organic attention is this getting right now? |
| Audience Fit | 1-5 | How well does this match the user's specific audience? |
| Timeliness | 1-3 | How time-sensitive? (3 = today, 2 = this week, 1 = anytime) |
| Content Gap | 1-3 | How well is this already covered? (3 = wide open, 1 = oversaturated) |
| Visual Potential | 1-3 | Can this be shown on screen in a compelling way? |
| Accessible | 1-3 | Can the user realistically create this content? (3 = easy, 1 = hard) |

**Maximum score: 22 points**

Only present findings that score 12 or higher. If fewer than 3 qualify, lower the threshold to 10 but note it.

### Step 4: Present the Top 3 Trends

For each of the top 3, provide:

```
## Trending Now in [Niche]

### #1: [Trend Title]
**Score:** XX/22 | **Window:** [How long this trend will last]
**What is happening:** [2-3 sentence summary with specifics — names, numbers, dates]
**Why it is trending:** [What triggered the spike in attention]
**Content angle:** [The specific video format and framing that would work best]
**Hook:** "[A ready-to-use opening line for this video]"
**Title idea:** "[A YouTube title that would work for this topic]"

### #2: [Trend Title]
**Score:** XX/22 | **Window:** [Duration]
**What is happening:** [Summary]
**Why it is trending:** [Trigger]
**Content angle:** [Format and framing]
**Hook:** "[Opening line]"
**Title idea:** "[Title]"

### #3: [Trend Title]
**Score:** XX/22 | **Window:** [Duration]
**What is happening:** [Summary]
**Why it is trending:** [Trigger]
**Content angle:** [Format and framing]
**Hook:** "[Opening line]"
**Title idea:** "[Title]"
```

### Step 5: Also On the Radar

Include 3-4 additional trends that are building but have not peaked yet. These are "watch and wait" items.

```
## Also On the Radar
- **[Topic]** — [One sentence on what it is and why to watch it]
- **[Topic]** — [One sentence]
- **[Topic]** — [One sentence]
```

### Step 6: The Recommendation

Be opinionated. Do not present all options as equal. Tell the user exactly what to make and why.

```
## My Pick: Make This One

**[Trend #X: Title]**

[2-3 sentences explaining why this is the best opportunity right now. Factor in timing, audience fit, competition, and the user's strengths. Be direct and confident.]
```

### Step 7: Offer Next Steps

After presenting, ask:
> "Want me to write a full script for the top pick, generate title variations, or do a deeper dive into any of these trends?"

## Rules

- Be specific. "AI is trending" is useless. "Claude added a new Projects feature and nobody has made a tutorial yet" is useful.
- Verify everything. Do not report trends based on a single ambiguous search result. Cross-reference across multiple sources.
- No filler. If only 2 strong trends exist, present 2. Do not pad with weak findings.
- Hooks and titles must be written at a 5th grade reading level. Short words, short sentences.
- Always note your sources so the user can verify the trends themselves.
- Prioritize trends where there is a clear content gap — something people want to know about but few creators have covered well yet.
