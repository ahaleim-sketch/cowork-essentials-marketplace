# Sales Page Writer

Write compelling sales page copy that converts visitors into buyers. Give it your offer details and get a complete, structured page following proven direct-response frameworks.

## Instructions

### Step 1: Understand the Offer

From the user's request, identify:

- **What's being sold** — Product, service, course, community, tool?
- **Who is it for** — Target audience. Be specific.
- **Price** — How much does it cost?
- **Main transformation** — What does the buyer get? What changes for them?
- **Key features/benefits** — What's included?
- **Objections** — What would stop someone from buying?
- **Social proof** — Testimonials, numbers, logos, results?
- **CTA** — What's the action? Buy now, join, book a call, start free trial?

Check CLAUDE.md for the user's brand voice and audience.

### Step 2: Write the Sales Page

Follow this proven structure. Every section serves a purpose:

```markdown
# [Headline — Big Promise or Transformation]

## [Subheadline — Who it's for + What they get]

---

### The Problem

[2-3 paragraphs. Describe the pain your audience is feeling RIGHT NOW. Be specific. Use their language. Make them feel seen. This section builds empathy and urgency.]

### What If There Was a Better Way?

[1-2 paragraphs. Bridge from the pain to the possibility. Don't pitch yet. Just open the door to a different outcome.]

---

### Introducing [Product/Service Name]

[1-2 paragraphs. What is it, in plain English? Not features. The transformation. What does their life/business look like AFTER?]

### What You Get

[List every deliverable with a benefit attached to each one]

- **[Feature]** — [Why it matters to them]
- **[Feature]** — [Why it matters]
- **[Feature]** — [Why it matters]
- **[Feature]** — [Why it matters]

### Who This Is For

- [Ideal customer description 1]
- [Ideal customer description 2]
- [Ideal customer description 3]

### Who This Is NOT For

- [Anti-customer 1 — filters out bad fits and builds trust]
- [Anti-customer 2]

---

### What Others Are Saying

> "[Testimonial 1]"
> — [Name, Title]

> "[Testimonial 2]"
> — [Name, Title]

[Only include real testimonials the user provides. Never fabricate.]

---

### Your Investment

**[Price]** — [Reframe the price against the value or the cost of not acting]

[If there are tiers or a guarantee, include them here]

### Frequently Asked Questions

**[Question 1]**
[Answer — address the real objection behind the question]

**[Question 2]**
[Answer]

**[Question 3]**
[Answer]

---

### [Final CTA Section]

[1-2 sentences restating the transformation. Then the button/link.]

**[CTA Button Text]** — [URL or instruction]
```

### Step 3: Writing Rules

- **Lead with transformation, not features.** Nobody buys a drill. They buy the hole in the wall.
- **Write at a 6th grade reading level.** Short sentences. Simple words. If a 12-year-old can't understand it, rewrite it.
- **One CTA.** Don't give them 5 things to do. One action. Repeat it 2-3 times throughout the page.
- **Address objections directly.** The FAQ section is your objection handler. Answer the real questions, not softballs.
- **Use "you" more than "we."** The page is about them, not you.
- **Never fabricate testimonials, results, or claims.** Use only what the user provides. If there's no social proof, skip that section and tell the user to add it later.

### Step 4: Save and Deliver

Save as: `Sales Page - [Product Name].md`

Offer: "Want me to shorten this for an email pitch, turn it into an HTML landing page, or write ad copy that drives to this page?"

## Rules

- Every claim must be based on information the user provides. No made-up statistics or results.
- The "Who This Is NOT For" section is not optional. It builds trust and filters tire-kickers.
- Keep paragraphs to 3 sentences max. Sales pages are scanned, not read.
- Always include at least 3 FAQ items that address real buying objections.
