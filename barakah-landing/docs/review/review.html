Blocker

Needed
Approval of this data store under Everett's Safeguards Rule and vendor program; an Everett-managed inbox for alerts; a retention period for lead records (consent records at least 4 years). Uploaded files are already set to delete after 30 days, and uploads stay off until you approve them.

What passes

No triggering terms

No rates, payment amounts, down-payment amounts or percentages, terms or finance charges. No claims about cost, savings, speed, approval odds or guarantees.

Restricted words

“Loan”, “interest” and “lender” appear only in the Loan Estimate document name, the worksheet labels, “Equal Housing Lender” and the Murabaha disclosure. No personal name in the page text.

Fair lending

“Open to all qualified applicants regardless of religion or national origin” is on the page, along with an “Open to all” label in the header.

Required disclosures present

Company legal name and NMLS #2129, advisor NMLS #2753124, Equal Housing statement, NMLS Consumer Access link, Murabaha disclosure, and a “not a quote” note under the worksheet that is always visible.

Consent design

Two separate boxes, unchecked by default and not required to submit. They name the legal entity and include STOP/HELP, message frequency, data rates and “not a condition of purchase”.

Consent evidence

For each box the server records checked or not, time, IP address, browser, page address, the exact text shown and its version, saved together with the lead.

No automated outreach

The system only emails the advisor. It never sends texts or places calls.

Worksheet is arithmetic only

It shows percentages of the visitor’s own figures and three neutral questions. No judgements, benchmarks, savings or program comparisons.

Personal data

None in web addresses, server logs, email subject lines or analytics. There are no analytics or tracking pixels on the page.

Loan Estimate files

Uploads are off until approved. When on, files are checked by content, stored privately under random names, and opened only through links that expire.
